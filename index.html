The WordPress Bet: Dan vs Chris

The claim. Chris says WordPress is the Encyclopaedia Britannica of the web: AI-native tools will overtake it, and its share of the web will fall faster from here. Dan, who has built WordPress sites for 12 years, says the decline levels off: WordPress stays the professional's tool, and AI plugs into it rather than replacing it.

The measure. WordPress's share of the websites real Chrome users visit: WordPress origins as a percentage of all origins in the Chrome UX Report (mobile), published monthly by HTTP Archive. Unvisited AI-generated junk does not count, which is why both sides accepted it.

The stake. A round of beers, Iain included.

The two arguments

Chris. Legacy platforms carry compatibility weight they cannot shed. A new entrant with none of it will build something more AI-aligned, fix the SEO plumbing, and leave the old tools behind. From now on the share goes down every year.

Dan. The fall so far is Wix, Squarespace and Shopify, not AI. AI-generated sites still cannot produce proper sitemaps, schema or consistent SEO, so serious businesses keep coming back to professionals, and professionals build on WordPress with AI alongside it.

HTTP Archive desktop crawl, for comparison. The bet is decided on mobile.

Method. Share = WordPress origins ÷ all origins, mobile, all ranks, all countries, from the HTTP Archive Technology Report API (cdn.httparchive.org/v1/adoption). HTTP Archive crawls the origins in the Chrome UX Report once a month and detects technologies with Wappalyzer. The page fetches the live series on every load and falls back to the snapshot committed in the repo (—). Each monthly figure is published a few weeks into the following month.

The record. Struck on a call on 11 September 2026 and on WhatsApp the same morning. The bet began as "will the share go up or down", until Chris found that it had already been falling since July 2022, so the two of them bet on the rate instead. Companion to the Bubble Bet, Karim vs Chris.
